## Naming

- **Networks for Humanity (NFH)** is the organisation. **Finternet** is one of NFH's *missions*. **UNITS** is the ledger/tokenisation platform of the Finternet mission.
- Preferred attribution: *"UNITS, the ledger of Finternet, a mission of Networks for Humanity (NFH)."*
- Write "Finternet" with only the F capitalised, and "UNITS" in capitals.
